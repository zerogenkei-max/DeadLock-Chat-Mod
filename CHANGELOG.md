# Deadlock Chat Mod patch notes

## 0.2.4 — New look and a bunch of fixes

Alright! Big one this time, I went through pretty much everything and cleaned it up. The menu got a new look so it fits in with Deadlock's own settings, and a lot of small stuff got fixed along the way.

- New look for the chat mod menu and the Windows customiser, cleaner and easier to use
- The settings button at the bottom of Settings is now called **CHAT MOD**
- Pings and messages in the hideout show up again
- Changing a setting and closing the menu right away doesn't lose it anymore
- Importing a theme code only changes the look and filters now, not everything else
- The **Visible for** timer goes up in 5 second steps after 10 seconds, so no more clicking 100 times to get to 2 minutes
- Chat runs lighter in long matches
- Word matching for pings now works in languages without spaces (Chinese, Japanese, Korean)
- The download is way smaller, it's just the mod now
- The Windows customiser doesn't get confused by switched off copies in Mod Manager anymore
- Checked with the latest Deadlock update (6861)

Big thanks to everyone that left a comment or sent me feedback, it really helps!

If you'd like the Windows customiser (.exe) for saving your settings between games, Shoot me a message on discord! (genkei_)

Your settings carry over when you update.
